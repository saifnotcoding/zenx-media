Frequently asked questions

Here are answers to the most common questions we get from creators and brands. If you’re wondering how it works, what it costs, or how we operate — you’ll find it here.
What kind of influencers do you work with?
We collaborate with carefully vetted creators across Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube — from rising micro-influencers to established personalities, depending on your campaign needs and audience goals.
Can ZenX Media manage the entire campaign for us?
Yes from influencer outreach and contract negotiation to content approvals and performance reporting, we handle everything start to finish so you can focus on results.
How are brand-influencer matches made?
We use a curated process that analyzes niche, content style, audience demographics, and brand alignment to ensure each collaboration feels organic and drives results.
How much does it cost to work with ZenX Media?
We operate on a performance-based model — creators don’t pay anything upfront. For brands, pricing depends on campaign scope, deliverables, and selected influencers. Get in touch for a custom quote.
